Self-hosted vector technology logos are displayed in context across the portfolio's hero, project cards, case studies, experience, and capabilities. They represent technology names, not vendor endorsements or certified credentials.

- AnyDesk, Cisco, Linux, Ubuntu, Debian, Proxmox, Docker, Fortinet, Palo Alto Networks, Wireshark, Snort, and GitHub: [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), CC0.
- Windows mark: [Devicon](https://github.com/devicons/devicon), MIT.
- Wazuh mark: [Wazuh Dashboard](https://github.com/wazuh/wazuh-dashboard/blob/main/src/core/server/core_app/assets/logos/wazuh_mark.svg), Wazuh trademark. See repository licensing; keep branding accurate.

All trademarks and names belong to their respective owners. No third-party branding suggests partnership, employment or certification.

## Education institution images

The education timeline uses the four institution logo images supplied by the portfolio owner on 2026-10-23: EMSI (engineering studies), OFPPT / ISMONTIC (network technician diploma), Université Abdelmalek Essaâdi (one year in Sciences de la Matière Physique), and the Moroccan Ministry of National Education (baccalaureate awarding authority). They are shown in the same order as the timeline but are not qualifications or endorsements beyond the corresponding written education entry.

A single compressed WebP image `public/images/education-institutions.webp` preserves their original visible branding, arranged as a 2×2 image sheet. `src/app/services-identity.css` displays individual quadrants with responsive cropping, and `src/components/Home.tsx` maps year to institution. Attribution: supplied by the owner; independent source URL and trademark provenance were not checked here. The ministry image accompanies the baccalaureate, not the lycée's own logo.

Institution marks must not be generated, recolored, or substituted. For later changes, use the institution's own original supplied image and keep the four year/brand mappings accurate.

- Zabbix: original owner-supplied ECG brand graphic from 2026-10-23, preserved and minimally resized/cropped for compact technology chips. Stored as `public/tech-logos/zabbix.webp`. Do not confuse with security-service certification or vendor endorsement.
